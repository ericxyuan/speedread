# Third-party software and design guidance

The source is an original implementation informed by the locally supplied Fluidity Core, Motion, Review and Components guidance. No Fluidity component source or CSS was copied into this project.

Runtime dependencies retain their upstream licences: React (MIT), Motion (MIT), Lucide (ISC), PDF.js (Apache-2.0), Mammoth (BSD-2-Clause), Tesseract.js and Tesseract.js-core (Apache-2.0), UTIF2 (MIT), CFB (Apache-2.0), idb (ISC), pinyin-pro (MIT), and @pinyin-pro/data (MIT). Consult each installed package for the complete licence and notices.

Bundled OCR assets under `public/ocr` come from Tesseract.js, Tesseract.js-core and the English, simplified Chinese and traditional Chinese tessdata distributions. PDF character maps and standard fonts under `public/pdf` come from PDF.js and retain their licence notices in those directories.
